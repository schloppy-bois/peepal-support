PeePal Support

PeePal is an iPhone diary for recording bathroom visits, drinks, and daily notes.

Using your diary

Record or edit a visit
On Home, tap Record a visit to save the current time. Open a visit to edit it, or use the Home menu to record an earlier visit.
Log drinks and daily notes
In History, tap Log a drink. To add a day’s notes, tap Daily notes next to its date.
Understand the Stats chart
Tap a day on the chart to see its visit count and average interval. Averages use gaps between consecutive visits recorded on the same day.
Back up or move your diary
Open the app menu, choose Backups, and save a full backup to Files. To bring it into PeePal, open Backups and choose Restore backup. History also exports a visit-only CSV.

Contact support

Email goodmeatsoftware@gmail.com for app help. Include your iOS version and what happened. Please leave out private diary records.

PeePal records the information you enter. Contact a healthcare professional for medical questions.
