PeePal Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Your diary

PeePal stores the visits, drinks, notes, and optional details you enter in app storage on your iPhone. It uses those entries to display your history, elapsed time, and statistics. There is no PeePal account or app-managed sync. PeePal does not transmit diary entries to Good Meat Software or use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Exports and backups

You choose whether to export a CSV or a full diary backup and where to save or share it. Your iPhone backup settings and the privacy terms of the destination you choose also apply to those copies.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain in the app until you remove them or delete the app and its local data. You can remove visits and drinks in History and clear daily notes. The app keeps deletion markers to prevent older backups from restoring removed visits or drinks. If a damaged diary is replaced by a restore, the original remains in local app storage as a recovery copy.

Delete copies you exported or backed up separately from the services or locations where you stored them.

Support and this website

If you email support, Good Meat Software receives your email address and whatever you include. We use support correspondence to respond, follow up, and meet applicable obligations. Our email provider processes these messages. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. Please leave private diary information out of your message. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements, at goodmeatsoftware@gmail.com.

These pages use no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
